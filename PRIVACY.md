# Privacy policy

gmail-tools is a command-line program that runs on your own computer. It reads Gmail and Google Drive data only through the `gog` and `gmailctl` programs, using Google accounts you sign in to yourself.

- **Data access:** It reads messages, labels, filters, and Drive files only when you run a command, and shows the results in your terminal.
- **Storage:** It stores no mail, files, or account data. Sign-in tokens stay in your system keyring, managed by `gog` and `gmailctl`.
- **Sharing:** It sends no data to the author or any third party. It talks only to Google's APIs.
- **Removal:** Remove access at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/zigai/gmail-tools/issues.
